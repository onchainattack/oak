# Audius — proxy admin address in storage slot 0 collided with the `Initializable` flags, so `initialize` could be called again and governance seized — Audius / Ethereum — 2022-07-23

**Loss:** 18,564,497 AUDIO moved from the community treasury ([Audius post-mortem](https://blog.audius.co/posts/audius-governance-takeover-post-mortem-7-23-22)), about \$6M at the time per [Rekt](https://rekt.news/audius-rekt) and [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/hackers-steal-6-million-from-blockchain-music-platform-audius/). The attacker sold the AUDIO in one Uniswap v2 transaction for 704 ETH (about \$1M) per [Rekt](https://rekt.news/audius-rekt); BleepingComputer says about \$1.07M. Audius says no new tokens were minted ([post-mortem](https://blog.audius.co/posts/audius-governance-takeover-post-mortem-7-23-22)).

**OAK Techniques observed:** **OAK-T9.003** (Governance Attack — *primary*). The proxy admin address in slot 0 overlapped OpenZeppelin `Initializable`'s `initialized` and `initializing` flags, making the `initializer()` modifier always succeed, so initialisation could be repeated to redefine voting, change the guardian and repoint staking contracts ([post-mortem](https://blog.audius.co/posts/audius-governance-takeover-post-mortem-7-23-22)). See [`techniques/T9.003-governance-attack.md`](../techniques/T9.003-governance-attack.md). **OAK-T7.001** (Mixer-Routed Hop — Rekt says funds were deposited to Tornado Cash about 10 hours after the sale).

**Attribution:** **pseudonymous** — attacker address [`0xa0c7BD318D69424603CBf91e9969870F21B8ab4c`](https://etherscan.io/address/0xa0c7BD318D69424603CBf91e9969870F21B8ab4c) per [Rekt](https://rekt.news/audius-rekt); no source names the person.

**Key teaching point:** **Test that no proxy slot overlaps initialiser flags, and use an initialiser guard that cannot be satisfied by an address stored in the same slot.** The post-mortem says audits in August 2020 (OpenZeppelin) and October 2021 (Kudelski) did not catch this ([post-mortem](https://blog.audius.co/posts/audius-governance-takeover-post-mortem-7-23-22)).

## Summary

Audius says its governance, staking and delegation contracts on Ethereum were compromised on 2022-07-23 through "a bug in the contract initialization code that allowed repeated invocations of the initialize functions" ([post-mortem](https://blog.audius.co/posts/audius-governance-takeover-post-mortem-7-23-22)). The attacker made two internal delegations of 10 trillion AUDIO each, passed a proposal on the resulting votes, and moved the treasury to a custom governance contract at [`0xbdbb5945f252bc3466a319cdcc3ee8056bf2e569`](https://etherscan.io/address/0xbdbb5945f252bc3466a319cdcc3ee8056bf2e569). The team froze the contracts and token, patched, and restored them (see timeline). The delegations were internal to the staking system and no new tokens were minted ([post-mortem](https://blog.audius.co/posts/audius-governance-takeover-post-mortem-7-23-22)).

## Root-cause evidence

- **Technique:** OAK-T9.003
- **Source:** <https://blog.audius.co/posts/audius-governance-takeover-post-mortem-7-23-22>; <https://rekt.news/audius-rekt>
- **Source type:** operator
