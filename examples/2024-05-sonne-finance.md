# Sonne Finance — empty soVELO market donated to and drained after a permissionless timelock execution — Sonne Finance / Optimism — 2024-05-14

**Loss:** **~\$20M** across several Sonne markets on Optimism (WETH, VELO, soVELO and wrapped USDC per [Rekt](https://rekt.news/sonne-finance-rekt); CertiK lists per-market amounts for soVELO, soWETH, soUSDC.e, soWBTC, sowstETH, soUSDT and soUSDC in its [incident analysis](https://www.certik.com/blog/sonne-finance-incident-analysis)). [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/news/ecosystems/2024-05-15-lending-protocol-sonne-finance-faces-20-million-exploit-pauses-markets-on-optimism-294508) also reports about \$20M. No recovery of the drained funds is reported by [Rekt](https://rekt.news/sonne-finance-rekt) or [CertiK](https://www.certik.com/blog/sonne-finance-incident-analysis). Both report that the MEV researcher Tony KΞ showed that adding \$100 of VELO to the soVELO pool prevented a further ~\$6.5M of loss; CertiK does not say whether this was a deliberate defence ([CertiK](https://www.certik.com/blog/sonne-finance-incident-analysis)). Sonne said it was ready to offer the exploiter a bounty for the return of funds ([The Block](https://www.theblock.co/news/ecosystems/2024-05-15-lending-protocol-sonne-finance-faces-20-million-exploit-pauses-markets-on-optimism-294508)).

**OAK Techniques observed:** **OAK-T9.011** (Precision-Loss / Rounding Attack — *primary*). CertiK describes the attacker inflating the exchange rate of a newly created, empty soVELO market by sending borrowed VELO straight to the contract (`totalCash` rose while `totalSupply` stayed at 2 wei), then using rounding in `redeemUnderlying` to redeem assets while paying fewer soTokens ([CertiK](https://www.certik.com/blog/sonne-finance-incident-analysis)). See [`techniques/T9.011-precision-loss-rounding-attack.md`](../techniques/T9.011-precision-loss-rounding-attack.md). **OAK-T9.007** (Fork-Substrate Vulnerability Not Mitigated — secondary). Sonne is a Compound v2 fork, and [Rekt](https://rekt.news/sonne-finance-rekt), [CertiK](https://www.certik.com/blog/sonne-finance-incident-analysis) and [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/news/ecosystems/2024-05-15-lending-protocol-sonne-finance-faces-20-million-exploit-pauses-markets-on-optimism-294508) all call the bug a known donation attack on Compound v2 forks, first seen in the Hundred Finance exploit of April 2023 (CertiK). See [`techniques/T9.007-fork-substrate-vulnerability-not-mitigated.md`](../techniques/T9.007-fork-substrate-vulnerability-not-mitigated.md).

**Attribution:** **pseudonymous** — attacker address [`0xae4a7cde7c99fb98b0d5fa414aa40f0300531f43`](https://optimistic.etherscan.io/address/0xae4a7cde7c99fb98b0d5fa414aa40f0300531f43) ([Rekt](https://rekt.news/sonne-finance-rekt)). Sonne said the investigation of the exploiter's identity was still going on ([The Block](https://www.theblock.co/news/ecosystems/2024-05-15-lending-protocol-sonne-finance-faces-20-million-exploit-pauses-markets-on-optimism-294508)).

**Key teaching point:** **Do not let a new lending market become exploitable between its creation and the step that protects it.** A governance proposal scheduled the new VELO market and a non-zero collateral factor behind a timelock; once the timelock expired anyone on Optimism could execute the queued steps, and the attacker executed them first and attacked the empty market ([Rekt](https://rekt.news/sonne-finance-rekt), [CertiK](https://www.certik.com/blog/sonne-finance-incident-analysis)). CertiK notes that multisig execution was permissionless on Optimism but not on Base, which let the attacker bypass Sonne's precautions. Rekt cites Daniel Von Fange's advice that timelock actions execute atomically, so that no one can choose which steps to run.

## Summary

Sonne Finance is a Compound v2 fork on Optimism. Per [CertiK](https://www.certik.com/blog/sonne-finance-incident-analysis), Sonne Improvement Proposal 15 (add VELO) opened on 4 May 2024 and closed on 7 May with 100% Yes votes; the team scheduled the required transactions on its multisig, which has a two-day timelock, and scheduled the collateral-factor changes to coincide. [Rekt](https://rekt.news/sonne-finance-rekt) reports that the proposal set the collateral factor of the VELO market to 35%, and that after the timelock expired anyone on Optimism could execute it. Sonne's own statement, as relayed by [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/news/ecosystems/2024-05-15-lending-protocol-sonne-finance-faces-20-million-exploit-pauses-markets-on-optimism-294508), says the exploiter executed four market-creation transactions once the timelock expired, then the collateral-factor transaction.

The attacker then used the empty soVELO market: it minted 2 wei of soVELO, sent about 35.47M borrowed VELO straight to the contract to inflate the exchange rate, borrowed 265 WETH against the 2 wei of soVELO, and used a rounding error in `redeemUnderlying` to get the VELO back while burning only 1 wei of soVELO ([CertiK](https://www.certik.com/blog/sonne-finance-incident-analysis)). CertiK says the process was repeated to drain the soUSDC and soWETH markets. The protocol paused all markets on Optimism; markets on Base stayed operational ([The Block](https://www.theblock.co/news/ecosystems/2024-05-15-lending-protocol-sonne-finance-faces-20-million-exploit-pauses-markets-on-optimism-294508)).

The sources do not agree on one detail: Rekt says the attacker bundled the proposal execution and the attack payload into a single transaction, while CertiK lists the collateral-factor transaction and the exploit transaction as two separate hashes (below).

## Root-cause evidence

- **Technique:** OAK-T9.011 (primary), OAK-T9.007
- **Source:** <https://www.certik.com/blog/sonne-finance-incident-analysis>; <https://rekt.news/sonne-finance-rekt>; <https://www.theblock.co/news/ecosystems/2024-05-15-lending-protocol-sonne-finance-faces-20-million-exploit-pauses-markets-on-optimism-294508>
- **Source type:** security-firm
- **Confidence:** corroborated
- **Evidence:** CertiK, Rekt and The Block agree that this was the known Compound v2 donation attack on a newly created empty market after the VELO proposal's timelock expired, and CertiK gives the call sequence and transaction hashes. Sonne's own post-mortem (on Medium) returned HTTP 403 and was not read; The Block relays Sonne's statement. Open: whether the attacker's execution of the queued steps and the attack were one transaction (Rekt) or two (CertiK), and the length of the timelock (CertiK and The Block: two days; Rekt: 24 hours).
