# Curio DAO — voting-power flaw in a MakerDAO-derived governance contract let the attacker run code that minted about 1 billion CGT — Curio / Ethereum — 2024-03-23

**Loss:** the nominal figure reported is about \$16M ([Halborn](https://www.halborn.com/blog/post/explained-the-curio-hack-march-2024), [REKT](https://rekt.news/curio-rekt)). REKT says the total is hard to calculate because CGT has limited market liquidity. [Merkle Science](https://blog.merklescience.com/hacktrack/hack-track-curiodao-flow-of-funds-analysis) tables show about \$140,498 taken on Ethereum (ETH, DAI, SKL, WETH) and \$37,246 on BNB Smart Chain, about \$177,745 combined. The sources do not reconcile the two figures. Curio says only its Ethereum side was affected (REKT).

**OAK Techniques observed:** **OAK-T9.003** (Governance Attack — *primary*: per Halborn the attacker acquired a small amount of CGT, used it to gain elevated voting privileges in the governance contract, ran `plot` to register a malicious exec library, then used delegatecall to mint about 1 billion CGT). See [`techniques/T9.003-governance-attack.md`](../techniques/T9.003-governance-attack.md). No flash loan is reported in the sources opened, so T9.002 and T16.001 are not claimed.

**Attribution:** **pseudonymous.** REKT lists one attacker address (below); no source opened names a person.

**Key teaching point:** **When forking governance code, test that a small stake cannot gain execution rights, and cap what the governance path can mint.** In this incident a small CGT holding was enough to take control of the exec path and mint about 1 billion tokens.

## Summary

Curio announced the exploit on 2024-03-23 ([REKT](https://rekt.news/curio-rekt)). Halborn describes the cause as a flaw in the voting and governance system of Curio's MakerDAO-derived contracts: the attacker obtained elevated voting privileges with a relatively small number of CGT tokens, used `plot` to define a malicious contract as the exec library, and minted about 1 billion CGT through delegatecalls. REKT, citing Hacken, adds that the attack started from a `cook` function of an attack contract using the IDSChief and IDSPause contracts. After the mint the attacker made token swaps and cross-chain transfers (Halborn, REKT). Curio published a two-stage recovery plan with a new token, CGT 2.0.
