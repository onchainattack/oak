# Agave / Hundred Finance reentrancy exploit — Gnosis Chain — 2022-03-15

**Loss:** Agave lost 2,116 ETH (about \$5.5M) and Hundred Finance 2,363 ETH (about \$6.2M), about \$11.7M in total `[rektagave]`; The Block rounds the total to about \$11M `[theblockagave]`. This file covers Agave; Hundred Finance was hit by the same root cause minutes later.
**OAK Techniques observed:** OAK-T9.005 (Reentrancy — primary; the attacker re-entered borrow logic through a post-transfer hook on Gnosis Chain tokens, borrowing more than the protocol recorded as debt `[rektagave]`). OAK-T1.007 (Token-2022 Transfer-Hook Abuse — original cross-standard analogy, retained as the original mapping).
**Attribution:** **unattributed** pseudonymous. Rekt lists separate attacker addresses for Agave and Hundred and names no individuals; none of the funds were reported returned, and the proceeds were sent to Tornado Cash `[rektagave]`. Sources cited here do not establish that the two attacks were by the same actor.
**Corrected 2026-10-11:** Agave is an Aave fork (not Compound V2) and Hundred Finance is the Compound fork; losses are about \$5.5M for Agave (the file said about \$5M across both); the "same attacker" claim and unsourced post-mortem/analyst references were removed.
**Key teaching point:** Lending-market forks deployed on a chain whose tokens run a callback on transfer inherit a reentrancy surface that the original deployment did not have. Sending borrowed tokens before updating the user's debt is the vulnerable ordering `[immunefihundred]`.

## Summary

On 2022-03-15 attackers exploited Agave (an Aave fork) and Hundred Finance (a Compound fork) on Gnosis Chain `[rektagave]`. Rekt attributes the vulnerability to the design of the xDAI token, which contains a `_callAfterTransfer()` function; the attacker used flash loans as initial collateral and nested borrow calls inside one another, borrowing more than the protocol could record in its debt balance `[rektagave]`. Immunefi traces both attacks to post-transfer hooks (`onTokenTransfer()`) in non-standard ERC-677 tokens on the bridge, which enabled reentrancy; for Hundred, the attacker deposited about \$2M in USDC as collateral and borrowed well over \$6M, and tokens were sent before the user's debt was updated `[immunefihundred]`.

Rekt reports that after a few hours the attacker sent the 2,116 ETH from Agave and 2,363 ETH from Hundred to Tornado Cash `[rektagave]`.
