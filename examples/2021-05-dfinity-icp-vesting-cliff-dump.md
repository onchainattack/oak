# DFINITY Internet Computer (ICP) — alleged insider sales of unlocked ICP to exchanges after the May 2021 launch — Internet Computer / Ethereum exchanges — 2021-05-10 onward

**Loss:** **Undisclosed as a realised loss.** ICP launched at \$630 in May 2021 and fell as low as \$27 within about a month, and was above \$60 by 2021-08-17 ([CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2021/08/17/dfinitys-icp-rebounds-even-as-wounds-fester-from-token-launch)). The plaintiffs' press release gives a high of over \$730 on 2021-05-10 and about \$60 on 2021-08-09 ([Roche Freedman via PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/roche-freedman-llp-brings-class-action-lawsuit-against-dfinity-usa-research-llc-for-engaging-in-insider-trading-and-other-securities-violations-resulting-in-billions-of-dollars-in-unlawful-sales-to-retail-investors-301353005.html)). No opened page says the price fall was caused by insider sales.

**OAK Techniques observed:** none — the opened pages carry allegations (an Arkham report that insiders and treasury sent ICP to exchanges, and a class action alleging insider trading), not an established mechanism. The plaintiffs allege insider-held tokens were "not locked up pursuant to vesting schedules" ([PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/roche-freedman-llp-brings-class-action-lawsuit-against-dfinity-usa-research-llc-for-engaging-in-insider-trading-and-other-securities-violations-resulting-in-billions-of-dollars-in-unlawful-sales-to-retail-investors-301353005.html)), so the vesting-cliff Technique, which needs a published unlock schedule, does not match what the sources say.

**Attribution:** **unattributed** — Arkham says addresses it linked to the treasury and insiders sent 18.9 million ICP to exchanges ([CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2021/08/17/dfinitys-icp-rebounds-even-as-wounds-fester-from-token-launch)). The addresses are not printed on any page opened here. [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/dfinity-icp-rebounds-even-wounds-190153511.html) says the 18.9 million figure is not independently verified. The defendants are named in a lawsuit; no finding by a court is reported.

**Key teaching point:** **Before buying a newly listed token, ask the issuer for the insider allocation and its lock-up terms in writing, and watch the labelled insider wallets for deposits to exchanges.** Here a volunteer said Dfinity did not disclose until 16 days after launch that venture investors and insiders had no vesting periods ([CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2021/08/17/dfinitys-icp-rebounds-even-as-wounds-fester-from-token-launch)); Arkham called the lack of transparency on distribution and unlock schedules contrary to industry best practices (same page).

## Summary

ICP began public trading in May 2021 at \$630, a \$45 billion market capitalisation, and fell 95% in the following month ([CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2021/08/17/dfinitys-icp-rebounds-even-as-wounds-fester-from-token-launch)). In June 2021 Arkham Intelligence published a report saying Dfinity quietly allowed the treasury and insiders to send 18.9 million ICP to exchanges (CoinDesk's wording of the report) ([CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2021/08/17/dfinitys-icp-rebounds-even-as-wounds-fester-from-token-launch)). A class action, *Valenti v. DFINITY USA Research LLC, DFINITY Foundation, and Dominic Williams*, Case No. 5:21-cv-06118 (N.D. Cal.), was announced on 2021-08-11 and alleges insider trading, securities fraud and the sale of unregistered securities; the press release says the suit was filed on 2021-08-10 and that "DFINITY and its insiders began to sell massive quantities of these ICP tokens to the public" ([PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/roche-freedman-llp-brings-class-action-lawsuit-against-dfinity-usa-research-llc-for-engaging-in-insider-trading-and-other-securities-violations-resulting-in-billions-of-dollars-in-unlawful-sales-to-retail-investors-301353005.html)). An earlier suit by a California resident named Dfinity, Polychain Capital and Andreessen Horowitz as defendants ([Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/lawsuit-claims-dfinity-icp-token-unregistered-security/)). Founder Dominic Williams told CoinDesk the criticism came largely from misinformation ([CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2021/08/17/dfinitys-icp-rebounds-even-as-wounds-fester-from-token-launch)); Yahoo Finance's copy of the CoinDesk story says neither the Foundation nor Williams responded to requests for comment, so the two accounts differ ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/dfinity-icp-rebounds-even-wounds-190153511.html)). The plaintiffs' claims are allegations, not court findings ([PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/roche-freedman-llp-brings-class-action-lawsuit-against-dfinity-usa-research-llc-for-engaging-in-insider-trading-and-other-securities-violations-resulting-in-billions-of-dollars-in-unlawful-sales-to-retail-investors-301353005.html)).
