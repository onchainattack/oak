# Genesis creditor — spoofed support calls and remote access used to steal about 4,100 BTC from one person — Bitcoin — 2024-08-19

**Loss:** The figures differ by source and basis. The [court information](https://storage.courtlistener.com/recap/gov.uscourts.dcd.274568/gov.uscourts.dcd.274568.2.0.pdf) (filed 2024-11-05) says approximately 4123.71908099 BTC, then worth approximately \$245,093,239. The [DOJ release of 2024-09-19](https://www.irs.gov/node/138796) (IRS-CI mirror) says over 4,100 BTC, worth more than \$230 million at the time. The [DOJ release of 2025-12-08](https://www.irs.gov/compliance/criminal-investigation/guilty-plea-and-superseding-indictment-announced-in-social-engineering-scheme-that-stole-263-million-in-cryptocurrency) (IRS-CI mirror) says over 4,100 Bitcoin, valued at \$263 million at the time and above \$368 million that week. [CryptoSlate](https://cryptoslate.com/zachxbt-helps-secure-arrests-in-243m-social-engineering-attack-on-one-person/) relays ZachXBT's \$243M total and a 4,064 BTC transfer. The pages do not explain the gaps. Recovery: [CryptoSlate](https://cryptoslate.com/zachxbt-helps-secure-arrests-in-243m-social-engineering-attack-on-one-person/) says the investigation contributed to the freezing of millions in assets, with no figure; [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/359078/teen-in-245m-bitcoin-heist-loses-bond-after-new-2m-crypto-theft-unsealed-court-docs-show) reports that one defendant forfeited about 30 watches, clothing and over \$36 million in ETH.

**OAK Techniques observed:** none — the mechanism is phone and remote-access social engineering of one individual, relayed from ZachXBT by CryptoSlate. The earlier version declared the engagement-weighted in-platform phishing Technique; its page covers phishing distributed through a platform's own ranking mechanic, which is not what any opened page describes, so it is removed. The laundering leg is in the sibling file [`2024-08-4064-btc-multi-hop-cross-chain-laundering.md`](2024-08-4064-btc-multi-hop-cross-chain-laundering.md).

**Attribution:** **confirmed** at the level of charges and pleas, as allegations until conviction. The [2024-09-19 DOJ release](https://www.irs.gov/node/138796) charges Malone Lam and Jeandiel Serrano. The [court information](https://storage.courtlistener.com/recap/gov.uscourts.dcd.274568/gov.uscourts.dcd.274568.2.0.pdf) charges Veer Chetal with conspiring with Lam and Serrano. [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/359078/teen-in-245m-bitcoin-heist-loses-bond-after-new-2m-crypto-theft-unsealed-court-docs-show) reports Chetal pleaded guilty to two counts. The [2025-12-08 DOJ release](https://www.irs.gov/compliance/criminal-investigation/guilty-plea-and-superseding-indictment-announced-in-social-engineering-scheme-that-stole-263-million-in-cryptocurrency) says a Second Superseding Indictment charges Nicholas Dellecave, Mustafa Ibrahim and Danish Zulfiqar with RICO conspiracy, and that Evan Tangeman is the ninth defendant to plead guilty. The earlier claim that only one defendant was arrested in September 2024 and the others were at large is contradicted by the 2024-09-19 release, which says both Lam and Serrano were arrested the night before.

**Key teaching point:** **A custodian's or exchange's support never asks a customer to reset two-factor authentication or install remote-access software on a call it did not initiate; treat any call that does as the attack.** Per [CryptoSlate](https://cryptoslate.com/zachxbt-helps-secure-arrests-in-243m-social-engineering-attack-on-one-person/), the victim reset two-factor authentication, installed AnyDesk and exposed the Bitcoin Core keys.

## Summary

The [court information](https://storage.courtlistener.com/recap/gov.uscourts.dcd.274568/gov.uscourts.dcd.274568.2.0.pdf) says that on or about 2024-08-18 the co-conspirators engaged in digital communications with a victim in Washington, D.C. and through them fraudulently obtained the means to transfer the Bitcoin. The 2024-08-19 date used in this file is the on-chain transfer time (04:05 UTC) given by [CryptoSlate](https://cryptoslate.com/zachxbt-helps-secure-arrests-in-243m-social-engineering-attack-on-one-person/); the two dates are consistent with a US evening call, but no opened page says so.

The method comes from ZachXBT, relayed by [CryptoSlate](https://cryptoslate.com/zachxbt-helps-secure-arrests-in-243m-social-engineering-attack-on-one-person/): the attackers called the victim, a Genesis creditor, impersonating Google Support from a spoofed phone number, which gave them access to personal accounts. They then posed as Gemini support, convinced the victim the exchange account was compromised, and got two-factor authentication reset and funds moved to an attacker-controlled wallet. The victim was then persuaded to use AnyDesk, and the attackers extracted private keys from Bitcoin Core. The DOJ pages do not describe the calls; they describe gaining fraudulent access to victim accounts and, per the [2024-09-19 release](https://www.irs.gov/node/138796), laundering through mixers and exchanges with peel chains, pass-through wallets and VPNs.

The charged group is described in the [2025-12-08 release](https://www.irs.gov/compliance/criminal-investigation/guilty-plea-and-superseding-indictment-announced-in-social-engineering-scheme-that-stole-263-million-in-cryptocurrency) as using stolen databases to target victims, with members acting as database hackers, organizers, target identifiers, callers and residential burglars targeting hardware wallets, from no later than October 2023 through at least May 2025. Proceeds went on travel, nightclubs, luxury cars, watches, jewelry, handbags and rental homes in Los Angeles and Miami ([2024-09-19 release](https://www.irs.gov/node/138796)).

Hour offsets, a three-way split of proceeds, a leaked screen-recording as key evidence, an arrest in Miami on a stated day, and biographical details in the earlier version were not supported by pages opened for this pass and are removed. The laundering path (venues, swaps, the reaction video) is in the [sibling file](2024-08-4064-btc-multi-hop-cross-chain-laundering.md).
