# SQUID (Squid Game token) — sell-blocked token cashed out by its creators — BNB Chain — 2021-11-01

**Loss:** **undisclosed per incident.** No opened page gives a total investor loss. [Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/next/2021/11/02/squid-game-cryptocurrency-loses-all-value-in-minutes-in-possible-2-8-million-rug-pull-scam) reports that a wallet linked to the creators "appeared to have cashed out almost €3 million worth of the token" and headlines €2.8 million. [TRM Labs](https://www.trmlabs.com/post/on-the-trail-of-the-squid-game-scammers) puts the three linked scams together at at least 35,025 BNB (about USD 19.3 million at the time); that is a cluster total, not the SQUID figure. No recovery is reported on the opened pages.

**OAK Techniques observed:** **OAK-T1.006** (Honeypot-by-Design — *primary*; per [CoinMarketCap](https://coinmarketcap.com/alexandria/article/i-lost-everything-how-squid-game-token-collapsed) an anti-dumping mechanism stopped most holders from selling and only holders of a second token, Marbles, could sell on the open market; TRM says the contracts restricted selling so that only the creators could sell. Whether the restriction was immutable bytecode or an admin-set parameter is undisclosed on the opened pages. See [`techniques/T1.006-honeypot-by-design.md`](../techniques/T1.006-honeypot-by-design.md)). **OAK-T7.001** (Mixer-Routed Hop — per TRM, 55 deposits into Tornado Cash on 2021-11-01, then consolidated). **OAK-T8.001** (Common-Funder Cluster Reuse — TRM linked the SQUID scammers to two other rug pulls).

**Attribution:** **pseudonymous** — no named individuals on the opened pages; TRM links the scammers to two earlier similar rug pulls. [CoinMarketCap](https://coinmarketcap.com/alexandria/article/i-lost-everything-how-squid-game-token-collapsed) notes the named CEO has no matching LinkedIn profile.

**Key teaching point:** **Simulate a sell from a non-deployer address before buying.** A token that cannot be sold by ordinary holders is detectable before entry, and the price chart in the days before the collapse shows nothing about it.

## Summary

SQUID launched in late October 2021 on BNB Chain as a play-to-earn token tied to the Netflix show, with no affiliation to it ([CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2021/11/01/squid-game-token-crashes-developers-say-theyve-left-the-project/)). It traded on PancakeSwap and DODO. [CoinMarketCap](https://coinmarketcap.com/alexandria/article/i-lost-everything-how-squid-game-token-collapsed) reports it launched at $0.01 and reached $4.42 after 72 hours. On 2021-11-01 the price peaked near $2,861.80 and then fell to $0.0007926 within five minutes (CoinMarketCap, London time). CoinDesk reports an address that BscScan labelled as reported to have been involved in a rug pull sold SQUID and cashed out millions of dollars in BNB. The website and Medium account went down, and the developers said on Telegram that they did not want to continue running the project. TRM later traced Tornado Cash use and linked the operators to two earlier scams.
