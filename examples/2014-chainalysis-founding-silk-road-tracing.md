# Chainalysis founding and Silk Road tracing — 2014

**Loss:** **Attribution event, not financial loss.** Chainalysis was co-founded in 2014 by Michael Gronager, Jan Møller and Jonathan Levin; Gronager was COO of Kraken when Kraken was hired to investigate the Mt. Gox hack, and the company grew out of that tracing work `[wiki]``[contrary]`. The earlier claim that the founders traced Silk Road seizure wallets to exchange accounts is not supported by the cited sources. The company became the dominant blockchain forensic vendor, serving law enforcement, exchanges, and financial institutions.
**OAK Techniques observed:** **OAK-T8.003** (On-Chain Transaction Graph De-Anonymization) — canonical commercial anchor. Chainalysis operationalized the academic address-clustering techniques from Meiklejohn et al. (2013) into a commercial platform that enabled law enforcement and exchange compliance teams to trace illicit cryptocurrency transactions to KYC-verified exchange accounts.
**Attribution:** **confirmed** — the company publicly documented its founders: Michael Gronager, Jan Møller and Jonathan Levin; founder titles differ between sources `[wiki]``[contrary]`.

**Key teaching point:** Chainalysis represents the commercialization of T8.003: the academic de-anonymization techniques became a production forensic platform that powers the vast majority of law-enforcement cryptocurrency attribution operations. The company's existence demonstrates that T8.003 is not merely an academic possibility but a commercial reality with operational law-enforcement outcomes.

**Corrected 2026-10-11:** the founding origin is the Mt. Gox investigation, not founder-run Silk Road wallet tracing; founder titles and the unsourced client-share claims were softened.

## Summary

Chainalysis was co-founded in 2014 by Michael Gronager, Jan Møller and Jonathan Levin `[wiki]`. Per Contrary Research, Levin met Gronager and Møller in early 2015 while they were working on tracing crypto lost from Mt. Gox, and the three teamed up `[contrary]`; Wikipedia says the company was formed to be the official investigator of the Mt. Gox hack, with Gronager then COO of Kraken `[wiki]`. Roles vary by source (Contrary lists Gronager as CEO, Levin as CSO and Møller as CTO; current Wikipedia lists Levin as CEO) `[wiki]``[contrary]`.

The founding insight: the Bitcoin transaction graph is pseudonymous but structurally traceable at scale, and law enforcement and financial institutions would pay for a commercial platform operationalizing address-clustering research. Wikipedia, citing a 2020 Thomson Reuters article, says Chainalysis helped law enforcement recover over \$1 billion from the Silk Road takedown `[wiki]`. Statements previously here about market share among U.S. federal agencies and exchanges, and about product names, are unverified and removed.
