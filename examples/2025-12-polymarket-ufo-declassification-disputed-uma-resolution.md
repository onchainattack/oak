## Summary

The market asked whether the Trump administration would declassify UFO files in 2025 (created 2025-04-17, per CryptoSlate). On 2025-12-04 the Pentagon's AARO released a video that Polymarket's own newsletter calls an "Unresolved UAP Report" ([The Oracle, 2025-12-18](https://news.polymarket.com/p/is-that-a-ufo)). Traders were split on whether it met the rules, which triggered the dispute process: a debate period, then a final vote by UMA tokenholders. The Oracle says the market resolved Yes. CryptoSlate gives the finalization as 00:27:58 UTC on 2025-12-10 after two proposals and two disputes.

The two sources disagree on the facts. The Oracle says experienced traders argued the AARO release was newly declassified videos on official government channels. CryptoSlate says no contemporaneous declassification notice was found and the only release it identified was AARO's "unresolved" imagery from 2022, which it says the market rules did not count. Both are given as reported.

CryptoSlate relays unnamed commenters who alleged whales bought near par (99 to 99.9 cents) before finalization and that token-weighted voting overrode trader consensus. It reports one trade of about \$615,000 at about 0.998 roughly 10 hours before resolution, by an unidentified trader. It gives no vote tallies. The earlier version of this file carried a 2026-10-11 correction note, a UMA market-cap comparison and a statement about refunds to NO holders; none of those is on an opened page, so all are removed. Related disputed Polymarket markets are in [`2025-03-polymarket-uma-ukraine-mineral-deal.md`](2025-03-polymarket-uma-ukraine-mineral-deal.md) and [`2025-07-polymarket-zelenskyy-suit.md`](2025-07-polymarket-zelenskyy-suit.md).
