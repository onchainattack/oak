# XRP — private keys of a Ripple co-founder's personal accounts used to move XRP to exchanges — XRP Ledger — 2024-01-30

**Loss:** **213M XRP (about \$112.5M)** per [CryptoSlate](https://cryptoslate.com/ripple-co-founder-chris-larsens-xrp-wallet-hacked-for-estimated-112-million/) and [crypto.news](https://crypto.news/zachxbt-tracks-over-100m-in-xrp-sent-to-exchanges-by-chris-larsen/), both relaying ZachXBT's tracing; CryptoSlate says the amount was not confirmed by the victim or Ripple-related individuals. A later report gives **283M XRP** for the same January 2024 theft, citing a forfeiture complaint ([Cointelegraph, 2025-03-07](https://cointelegraph.com/news/ripple-co-founder-larsen-150m-xrp-theft-password-manager-breach)). The figures are not reconciled on the opened pages. Per [crypto.news](https://crypto.news/zachxbt-tracks-over-100m-in-xrp-sent-to-exchanges-by-chris-larsen/), the victim said a significant portion was frozen and nearly all affected funds had been converted out of XRP. Recovery came later: [Krebs](https://krebsonsecurity.com/2025/03/feds-link-150m-cyberheist-to-2022-lastpass-hacks/) says the March 2025 forfeiture complaint concerns about \$24M of cryptocurrency clawed back, and [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/cryptocurrency-theft-attacks-traced-to-2022-lastpass-breach/) says the Secret Service seized more than \$23M in 2025. The two figures are not reconciled on the opened pages.

**OAK Techniques observed:** **OAK-T11.006.001** (User-Initiated Plaintext-Equivalent Seed Storage, *primary*, reported). ZachXBT said the theft resulted from storing private keys in the LastPass password manager, citing a forfeiture complaint filed by US law enforcement. [Krebs](https://krebsonsecurity.com/2025/03/feds-link-150m-cyberheist-to-2022-lastpass-hacks/) reports the complaint was filed 2025-03-06 in northern California and says there was probable cause that the same attackers used a stolen password from the victim's password manager account. LastPass disputes a proven link: its law enforcement partners "have not made us aware of any conclusive evidence that connects any crypto thefts to our incident" (same page). The cause is reported, not established; the wider cohort is in [`2022-12-lastpass-vault-cohort.md`](2022-12-lastpass-vault-cohort.md). See [`techniques/T11.006.001-user-initiated-plaintext-seed-storage.md`](../techniques/T11.006.001-user-initiated-plaintext-seed-storage.md). In January 2024 the vector was unestablished: [CryptoSlate](https://cryptoslate.com/ripple-co-founder-chris-larsens-xrp-wallet-hacked-for-estimated-112-million/) says ZachXBT speculated about compromised keys and the victim did not describe the method. The exchange off-ramp is mapped to no Technique: the opened pages name the exchanges but not the deposit-address structure.

**Attribution:** **unattributed.** No page opened for this file names the thief. Exchanges named as destinations: see the timeline.

**Key teaching point:** **Do not keep signing keys in a password manager, and watch known high-value accounts for outflows.** A WhiteBIT alert to the XRP Ledger Foundation team on 2024-01-30 was the first detection reported on the opened pages ([CryptoSlate](https://cryptoslate.com/ripple-co-founder-chris-larsens-xrp-wallet-hacked-for-estimated-112-million/)). Exchanges froze part of the funds only after they were told which addresses to block.

## Summary

The accounts were the personal XRP accounts of a Ripple co-founder, not Ripple's corporate wallets: he said so in his own post, and said Ripple wallets were never compromised ([CryptoSlate](https://cryptoslate.com/ripple-co-founder-chris-larsens-xrp-wallet-hacked-for-estimated-112-million/); [crypto.news](https://crypto.news/zachxbt-tracks-over-100m-in-xrp-sent-to-exchanges-by-chris-larsen/)). On 2024-01-30 the exchange WhiteBIT alerted the XRP Ledger Foundation team to suspicious deposits, and the team informed the victim. On 2024-01-31 ZachXBT published the transfers and the victim posted a statement. ZachXBT traced the XRP to MEXC, Gate, Binance, Kraken, OKX, HTX and HitBTC. In March 2025 ZachXBT shared a screenshot of a forfeiture complaint that he said tied the theft to keys stored in LastPass ([Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/ripple-co-founder-larsen-150m-xrp-theft-password-manager-breach)); Krebs reports the complaint was filed 2025-03-06 in northern California ([Krebs](https://krebsonsecurity.com/2025/03/feds-link-150m-cyberheist-to-2022-lastpass-hacks/)).

- **Source:** <https://cointelegraph.com/news/ripple-co-founder-larsen-150m-xrp-theft-password-manager-breach>; <https://krebsonsecurity.com/2025/03/feds-link-150m-cyberheist-to-2022-lastpass-hacks/>; <https://cryptoslate.com/ripple-co-founder-chris-larsens-xrp-wallet-hacked-for-estimated-112-million/>; <https://crypto.news/zachxbt-tracks-over-100m-in-xrp-sent-to-exchanges-by-chris-larsen/>
