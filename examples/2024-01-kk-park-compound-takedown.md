## Summary

KK Park was a forced-labour scam compound located on the Myanmar side of the Myanmar-Thailand border, operating from approximately 2021 and still active into 2025. The compound housed on the order of 2,000 or more workers (the October 2025 raid detained 2,198 people), predominantly from China, Taiwan, Vietnam, Thailand, Malaysia, and other Southeast Asian countries, who were forced to operate romance-scam and friendship-scam feeder narratives targeting victims worldwide.

The compound's operational model was the canonical T11.005.003 template:

1. **Labour-force scale:** Thousands of trafficked workers operated feeder narratives across dating apps, social media, and messaging platforms in multiple languages, at industrial throughput.
2. **Multi-layer fraud stacking:** The compound operated fake-CEX platforms (T11.005.001), fake-custodian / asset-manager frauds (T11.005.002), and fake-recovery services targeting previous victims of the compound's own platforms — a closed-loop fraud ecosystem.
3. **Jurisdictional arbitrage:** The compound exploited the contested jurisdiction of the Myanmar-Thailand border region, where law-enforcement cooperation was limited and extradition channels with victim-residence jurisdictions (U.S., EU, OECD) were functionally absent.
4. **Integrated laundering:** Victim deposits in BTC, ETH, and USDT (predominantly on Tron) were funnelled through laundering infrastructure adjacent to the compound's operators.

The compound was the subject of extensive investigative journalism (BBC, Reuters) and UNODC/OHCHR reporting. Satellite imagery documented the compound's physical expansion from 2021 through 2024. No 2024 disruption of KK Park was found in the sources reviewed. The documented enforcement events are the 2025 crackdowns: a February 2025 Thai-led pressure campaign (cross-border internet and power cuts; an AFP report cites roughly 7,000 workers repatriated across the border region, not KK Park alone) and the Myanmar military's October 2025 raid, which detained 2,198 people at KK Park and was described by locals and experts as a staged public-relations exercise. The operational model is reported to persist and replicate elsewhere (Shwe Kokko in Myanmar, O-Smach Resort in Cambodia).

KK Park is the canonical per-compound T11.005.003 anchor, complementing the Huione/Prince Group federal-enforcement anchor at `examples/2025-10-huione-compound-fraud-cohort.md`. Together they establish that the compound-operated fraud model is a recurring structural phenomenon across independent Southeast Asian compounds, not a single-entity aberration.

## Realised extraction

No source reviewed quantifies aggregate victim deposits for KK Park; any total is unverified. Recovery is generally difficult for compound-operated fraud because funds move through compound-adjacent laundering infrastructure.
