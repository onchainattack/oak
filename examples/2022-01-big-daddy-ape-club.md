# Big Daddy Ape Club NFT rug pull — Solana — 2022-01-11

**Loss:** 9,136 SOL, about \$1.3M at the time, sent by would-be collectors to mint the Big Daddy Ape Club (BDAC) NFTs on Solana; no NFTs were delivered `[decryptbdac]`. The project was billed as 2,222 ape-themed NFTs listed via Solanart `[decryptbdac]`. Decrypt dates the rug pull to January 11, 2022 `[decryptbdac]`. No recovery is documented in the cited sources.
**OAK Techniques observed:** **OAK-T1.003** broadly construed as the residual-authority pattern (the operators controlled the wallet receiving the mint proceeds); **OAK-T12.x** (NFT rug-pull sub-class) as the structural classification of an NFT-mint-proceeds exit; **OAK-T5.001** (Hard LP Drain / Rug Pull — all proceeds taken in a single exit, structurally the hard-rug shape).
**Attribution:** **pseudonymous**. The project's founder passed an identity check by Civic (see below), but the cited sources do not name an individual; Civic stated it was cooperating with law enforcement `[decryptbdac]`.
**Corrected 2026-10-11:** removed the invented "Civik" researcher thread, the SDNY civil complaint, the cross-chain/cluster-reuse claims and the "Frosties on Solana the day before" framing (Frosties was an Ethereum NFT project); added the Civic identity-verification angle and the Decrypt-sourced facts.
**Key teaching point:** **Identity verification of a project founder is not a safeguard for buyers' funds.** BDAC was announced as "Verified by Civic Pass" on 2021-12-22, covering control of the Twitter handle and domain, ID capture and a 3D face scan of the founder; Civic's CEO said Civic does not endorse projects or perform due diligence beyond identity verification `[decryptbdac]`. The founder nonetheless took the mint proceeds. Mint buyers had no on-chain constraint on where their SOL went after payment.

## Summary

BDAC was a planned 2,222-piece ape-themed NFT collection on Solana, to be listed on Solanart `[decryptbdac]`. Digital artist Faith Orr reported that the project's Discord was locked a few hours before the mint, and victims said they received no NFTs after paying; the Twitter account, Discord and website then went dark `[decryptbdac]`. Orr called it an especially brutal move, since most rug pulls at least leave buyers holding NFTs `[decryptbdac]`. Solanart said it was deleting the collection from its grid, noting that the project had been approved through Civic verification but "decided to scam anyway" `[decryptbdac]`.

Binance reportedly blocked accounts that received some of the funds, per researcher Mert, and said it was investigating without commenting on specific law-enforcement engagements `[decryptbdac]`. Civic said it was cooperating with law enforcement and was in direct contact with U.S.-based authorities `[decryptbdac]`.
