# BTER — 7,170 BTC taken from exchange cold wallets, cause undisclosed — Bitcoin exchange — 2015-02-14

**Loss:** **7,170 BTC**, reported as about \$1.75M in the article, which also prices one BTC at about \$270; those two figures do not match (7,170 BTC at \$270 is about \$1.9M) ([The Hacker News, 2015-02-16](https://thehackernews.com/2015/02/bter-bitcoin-exchange-hacked.html)). BTER offered a bounty of **720 BTC** to anyone who helps recover the coins ([The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2015/02/bter-bitcoin-exchange-hacked.html)). No return of funds is reported in the source.

**OAK Techniques observed:** none — the source places the loss in the Custody and Signing Infrastructure tactic (OAK-T11) but gives no mechanism, so no Technique can be chosen. The article says the bitcoin was taken from the exchange's cold wallets and does not say how they were compromised ([The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2015/02/bter-bitcoin-exchange-hacked.html)); no sub-technique can be chosen from this source.

**Attribution:** **unattributed** — the article identifies no suspects ([The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2015/02/bter-bitcoin-exchange-hacked.html)).

**Key teaching point:** **"Cold" storage only helps if the people and systems that can move the coins are as hard to reach as the keys.** In this case the coins left cold wallets and the cause is undisclosed, so a defender cannot tell which control failed ([The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2015/02/bter-bitcoin-exchange-hacked.html)).

## Summary

BTER announced on 2015-02-14 that 7,170 BTC had been stolen from its cold wallets ([The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2015/02/bter-bitcoin-exchange-hacked.html)). The stolen bitcoin was moved in an on-chain transaction and then appeared to be split across several wallets ([The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2015/02/bter-bitcoin-exchange-hacked.html)). BTER shut down all wallets, said withdrawals of unaffected coins would be arranged later, and according to Weibo said it was working with law enforcement ([The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2015/02/bter-bitcoin-exchange-hacked.html)). The article notes BTER had also been hacked in August 2014, when about \$1.65M in NXT was reportedly stolen, most of it recovered per CoinDesk ([The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2015/02/bter-bitcoin-exchange-hacked.html)).
