# Pixelmon — reveal art far below the promised quality after a \$70M mint — Pixelmon / Ethereum — 2022-02-26

**Loss:** the project raised about \$70M at its 2022-02-07 mint, per [CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2022/02/28/pixelmon-nft-reveal-disappoints-with-hilariously-ugly-art) and [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/93964/70-million-pixelmon-founder-calls-artwork-reveal-horrible-mistake). Buyers paid up to 3 ETH each (about \$9,200 per CoinDesk, about \$8,100 per Decrypt); Decrypt says the price started at 3 ETH and fell through a Dutch auction. By Monday 2022-02-28 the floor on OpenSea was about 0.4 ETH (about \$1,100) per CoinDesk; by September 2022 the cheapest listings started at 0.36 ETH (about \$475) per [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/110542/nft-game-pixelmon-attempts-comeback-after-70m-horrible-art-). The mint supply is not stated on the pages opened. No source opened reports a refund.

**OAK Techniques observed:** none — the sources describe a disappointing reveal and a founder apology, not a contract exploit, theft or a documented treasury exit, so no Technique page fits. An earlier version of this file mapped it to T12.x, T3.003, T5.005 and T17.001; none of those matches a mechanism on a page opened.

**Attribution:** **unattributed.** No wrongdoing by any person is established by the sources opened. The project lead is named as "Syber" on social media in [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/93964/70-million-pixelmon-founder-calls-artwork-reveal-horrible-mistake); no legal name is given there.

**Key teaching point:** **Do not release on a date set by hype: show the art on the real assets before the mint and reveal only when the holder-facing art is final.** CoinDesk reports the project lead said the team felt pressure to hold the reveal before the art was ready, and Decrypt reports he said the assets bought for the prototype were not used in the NFTs ([CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2022/02/28/pixelmon-nft-reveal-disappoints-with-hilariously-ugly-art), [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/93964/70-million-pixelmon-founder-calls-artwork-reveal-horrible-mistake)).

## Summary

Pixelmon sold out its Generation 1 mint on 2022-02-07, raising about \$70M ([CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2022/02/28/pixelmon-nft-reveal-disappoints-with-hilariously-ugly-art)). Its roadmap pledged the largest and highest quality game the NFT space had seen, and holder perks of land airdrops, presale access and staking rewards (same source). The reveal came on the weekend of 2022-02-26; holders posted images of creatures hatching half-submerged in grass, and one received what looked like an empty patch of grass. The project lead called the reveal "unacceptable" and "a horrible mistake", and said the NFTs are updateable and would be made better ([Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/93964/70-million-pixelmon-founder-calls-artwork-reveal-horrible-mistake)). In a Discord AMA he apologised and blamed poor execution and a really small team ([CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2022/02/28/pixelmon-nft-reveal-disappoints-with-hilariously-ugly-art)). Later in 2022 LiquidX acquired a 60% stake and took over development, and the roadmap moved alpha testing to Q1 2023 ([Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/110542/nft-game-pixelmon-attempts-comeback-after-70m-horrible-art-)).

An earlier version of this file dated the event to 2022-05, gave a supply of 7,777, described a treasury drawn down over 2022 to 2024, and said the founder was publicly identified by name. None of these is on a page opened in the 2026-11 source pass. The filename date prefix (2022-05) does not match the 2022-02 events.
