# Murad — 11 wallets holding about \$24M in meme coins linked to a promoter by on-chain tracing — Ethereum / Solana — 2024-10-09

**Loss:** none reported. No source describes a victim loss, a theft or a sale. [ZachXBT](https://x.com/zachxbt/status/1843940648430493906) reports that 11 wallets tied to the meme coin promoter @MustStopMurad hold about \$24M in meme coins on Ethereum and Solana.

**OAK Techniques observed:** none — no opened page shows a sale, manipulation, theft or fraud. The sources describe a disclosure of wallet holdings and a concern about a promoter controlling supply while promoting coins; ZachXBT's stated purpose is "so the community can monitor his future activity" ([ZachXBT](https://x.com/zachxbt/status/1843940648430493906)). A promote-and-sell Technique would need evidence of outflows during promotion, which the sources do not give.

**Attribution:** **inferred-strong** for the link between the wallets and the promoter, as ZachXBT's own assessment ("high confidence", [ZachXBT](https://x.com/zachxbt/status/1843940648430493906)). The wallets are linked by a shared Ethereum funding source and other signals (see Summary). No operator, court or exchange confirms it, and the promoter's response is not in the sources read.

**Key teaching point:** **Splitting holdings across wallets that share one funder does not hide them.** The linking evidence in the sources is a common funding source, holdings similar to the promoter's public posts, repeated use of one bridge, and a funding source tied to a team address of a platform the promoter invested in ([Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/zachxbt-reveals-claimed-murad-mahmudov-24-million-memecoin-wallets), relaying ZachXBT). A defender or community monitoring a promoted token can cluster wallets by funder in the same way.

## Summary

On 2024-10-09 09:04 UTC, [ZachXBT posted on X](https://x.com/zachxbt/status/1843940648430493906) that he had found 11 high confidence wallets tied to @MustStopMurad holding about \$24M in meme coins on Ethereum and Solana. The post carries two images: a Zapper view of an 11-address group with a net worth of \$24.0M (\$18.0M on Ethereum, \$6.01M on Solana), with the largest positions SPX (\$17.0M), GIGA (\$3.50M) and RETARDIO (\$1.06M), and a TRM graph of the funding relationships. Both images show addresses only in shortened form. News outlets give the 8th or 10th of October for the post and article dates; the X post's own timestamp is 2024-10-09.

[Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/zachxbt-reveals-claimed-murad-mahmudov-24-million-memecoin-wallets) reports that ZachXBT said the wallets were funded from the same source on Ethereum; that the Solana addresses' funding source was directly tied to a team address of the platform STFX, in which the promoter is an investor; that each Solana address kept using deBridge; and that a \$449,886 purchase of MINI coins on July 16 came about an hour before the promoter posted on X about acquiring 1% of that token's supply. The same article says the promoter did not immediately respond to a request for comment. [Protos](https://protos.com/zachxbt-reveals-wallets-of-analyst-murad-mahmudov-angers-memecoiners/) reports ZachXBT's concern that the promoter makes very bold predictions about micro-cap meme coins to thousands of followers while controlling the supply. Neither article reports a sale or dump by these wallets. Reactions were split between accusations of doxxing and the view that the data was already public.
