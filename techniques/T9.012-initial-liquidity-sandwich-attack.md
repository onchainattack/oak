# OAK-T9.012 — Initial Liquidity Sandwich Attack (DEPRECATED)

**Maturity:** deprecated
**Superseded by:** none — the nearby real patterns are returned to [`TAXONOMY-GAPS.md`](../TAXONOMY-GAPS.md) as candidates
**Deprecated:** 2026-10-11

## Why this ID is deprecated

The Technique described an attacker who **sandwiches a token deployer's `addLiquidity` transaction**: buy "at the zero-reserve price" before the pool is seeded, then sell into the freshly added liquidity. That is not possible as written — there is nothing to buy from a pool that has no liquidity yet — and no source located in the 2026-10-11 source pass documents an incident of that shape.

Its worked examples were three cohort files (an initial-liquidity sandwich cohort, a MEV-sandwich cohort and a PancakeSwap token-launch MEV cohort). All three cited reports that could not be found, carried unsourced per-launch figures, and were quarantined on 2026-10-11 (see `CORRECTIONS.md`). The pump.fun sniping example once mapped here is documented by its source (Helius) as ordinary swap sandwiching and now maps to OAK-T5.004. With no confirmed incident and a mechanism that does not hold, the ID does not meet OAK's bar.

The ID is retained and not reused, per `CONTRIBUTING.md`.

## What is real nearby

Two token-launch patterns are real and are recorded as TAXONOMY-GAPS candidates, to be minted under new IDs once an incident is documented from a source:

- **Launch sniping** — a bot buys in the same block as, or the block after, the deployer's `addLiquidity`, ahead of other buyers. It is front-running of the first buyers, not of the liquidity provider.
- **Pool pre-creation at a skewed ratio** — someone creates the pair, or seeds it with dust at an attacker-chosen price, before the deployer's `addLiquidity`, so the deployer's liquidity is added at the attacker's ratio unless the deployer's minimum-amount checks reject it.

Ordinary sandwiching of later swaps in a new token is already covered by **OAK-T5.004 (Sandwich MEV Extraction)**; slippage-band abuse by **OAK-T9.013**.

## What went wrong, for the record

The Technique, its spec and its three cohort anchors were all part of the v0.1 bulk release, written to the same unsourced pattern: a plausible-sounding mechanism, confident per-launch ETH and BNB ranges, and citations to vendor reports that do not exist. None of them were checked against a source until the 2026-10-11 pass, which is the same failure behind OAK-T10.008.
